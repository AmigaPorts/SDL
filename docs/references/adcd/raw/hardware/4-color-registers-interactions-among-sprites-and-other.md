# 4 / Color Registers / Interactions Among Sprites and Other Objects


Playfields share the display with sprites. Chapter 7, "System Control
Hardware," shows how playfields can be given different video
 [display priorities](../Hardware_Manual_guide/node0155.html)  relative to the sprites and how playfields can
 [collide](../Hardware_Manual_guide/node015A.html)  with (overlap) the sprites or each other.
